# Amrit Dairy Farms - Customer & Delivery Partner User Manual

---

# Part 1: Customer Guide

Welcome to Amrit Dairy Farms! Enjoy pure, untouched A2 Gir cow milk and organic dairy delivered to your door before 7:00 AM every single morning.

---

## 1. Quick Sign Up & Login (Phone OTP)

1. Open the mobile app or visit `http://localhost:3000/#customer`.
2. Tap **Login / Sign Up** and enter your 10-digit mobile number.
3. Tap **Send OTP**.
4. Enter the 4-digit verification code (for demo testing, enter `1234`).
5. Your prepaid dairy wallet is automatically credited with a **₹250 Welcome Bonus**!

---

## 2. Check Delivery in Your PIN Code

1. On the home page, enter your 6-digit postal code (e.g. `560034`).
2. Tap **Check PIN**.
3. If serviceable, you will see your delivery hub and guaranteed arrival time (before 7:00 AM).

---

## 3. Starting a Subscription

1. Find your preferred milk or dairy product (e.g., *Pure A2 Desi Gir Cow Milk*).
2. Tap **📅 Subscribe**.
3. Choose:
   - **Frequency:** Daily Morning, Alternate Days, Custom Days (Mon/Wed/Fri), or Monthly Fixed Saver.
   - **Quantity:** 0.5L, 1L, 2L, or 3L.
   - **Time Slot:** Morning (5:30 AM - 7:30 AM) or Evening (5:30 PM - 7:30 PM).
   - **Bottle Preference:** Sterilized Returnable Glass Bottle (Recommended) or Eco Pouch.
   - **Start Date:** Tomorrow morning by default.
4. Tap **Confirm & Activate Subscription**.

---

## 4. Vacation Pause / Skip Delivery Calendar

Going out of town? You never have to call customer care or message your delivery driver.

1. Open the **Customer App → Subscriptions & Pause** tab.
2. Under **Pause / Skip Delivery Calendar**, view the next 14 days.
3. Tap on any date to pause:
   - 🟢 **Green cell:** Milk will be delivered.
   - 🔴 **Red cell (struck through):** Delivery paused. Your wallet will **not** be charged.
4. You can pause or unpause anytime until 10:00 PM the previous evening.

---

## 5. Wallet Recharge & Passbook

1. Tap **💳 Wallet & Recharge**.
2. View your live prepaid balance.
3. Tap **+ ₹500**, **+ ₹1,000**, or **+ ₹2,000** for one-tap recharge via UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) or Credit/Debit Card.
4. The Passbook table displays a transparent ledger of every morning bottle delivery deduction and recharge credit.

---

## 6. Live Morning Delivery Tracker

1. Tap **🚚 Live Delivery Status**.
2. Track your morning bottle across 4 milestone stages:
   1. **Milked at Farm:** 4:30 AM at Nelamangala pastures.
   2. **Rapidly Chilled to 4°C:** Halts bacterial growth without boiling.
   3. **Lab Tested Purity:** Fat & SNF percentages verified.
   4. **Out for Delivery:** Real-time driver name and vehicle number shown.
   5. **Delivered:** Chilled bottle placed at your door.

---

## 7. Glass Bottle Return Policy

- Our returnable glass bottles are made of thick, food-grade tempered glass.
- Simply rinse yesterday's bottle with tap water and leave it outside your door in the insulated bag.
- Our driver collects the empty bottle while delivering today's fresh milk.
- Zero plastic waste, 100% eco-friendly!

---

## 8. Referrals & Discounts

- Share your unique referral code (e.g., `ANANYA20`) with neighbors and friends.
- When they subscribe, both of you receive **₹100** credited to your dairy wallets.
- Use discount coupon `FRESH50` for 20% OFF on Bilona Ghee and Malai Paneer.

# Part 2: Delivery Partner Guide

---

## 1. Opening Route Manifest

1. Access the partner console at `http://localhost:3000/#delivery`.
2. Select your slot: **Morning (5:30 - 7:30 AM)** or **Evening (5:30 - 7:30 PM)**.
3. The app displays stops sequentially by neighborhood to optimize your EV route.

---

## 2. Completing a Delivery Stop

1. Stop at the customer's apartment / villa door.
2. Verify order items (e.g., `1L Pure A2 Gir Cow Milk`).
3. Place chilled bottle at doorstep.
4. Tap **✅ Mark Delivered**.
5. When prompted, enter the number of empty return glass bottles collected from the customer.
6. The stop turns green and the customer receives an instant delivery confirmation push notification.

---

## 3. Customer Not at Home / Gate Locked

1. If the door bell is unanswered or building gate is closed, tap **🚪 Door Locked / Skip**.
2. The stop is logged and customer care is automatically alerted.

---

## 4. Collecting UPI / Cash Payments

1. For one-time orders or customers paying on delivery, tap **📱 Collect UPI / QR**.
2. Present the on-screen dynamic QR code for the customer to scan with PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm.
3. Payment confirmation reflects immediately.
